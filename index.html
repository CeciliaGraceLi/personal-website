About Me

I became interested in analytics because I enjoy understanding why people make decisions and how data can improve them. Studying psychology taught me how people think, economics taught me how incentives shape behavior, and data science gave me the tools to test ideas with data.

Conversations with professionals at Fidelity introduced me to people analytics, where employee and organizational data can be used to predict trends and improve workplace decisions. That combination of psychology, business, and analytics is exactly the type of work I hope to pursue.

I recently completed Boston College's Summer Catalyst Program, an intensive introduction to financial analysis, marketing strategy, and business decision-making. Outside of coursework, I'm the lead singer and guitarist in a rock band, and I box and shoot photography in my free time — habits that keep me curious and comfortable being a beginner at new things, which is exactly the mindset I bring to data work too.
